## Remaining / 未収録

Missing entries do not mean no model exists. Manufacturer identity, official N-scale product evidence, and code/packaging verification are required. Similar trains, HO models, paper downloads and aftermarket decals alone do not establish a complete N-scale product. Coverage notes preserve specific reasons for each unresolved preset.
